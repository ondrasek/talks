Colophon

These decks are built by an agentic pipeline running against a private Obsidian vault of about 7,000 notes — reading notes, research reports and working memory, cross-linked and kept under version control.

A talk is drafted from notes that already exist. A panel of critics then tries to fail the draft, and it is rewritten until none of them can. They check that every claim carries a source, that each cited span says what it is quoted as saying, and that the register holds from one slide to the next. A headless browser measures every element on every slide to confirm nothing is painted off the bottom edge, because a slide does not scroll and text lost that way is lost silently.

Two kinds of citation appear. [S] is external and carries a URL you can follow. [V] is a note from the vault itself, which stays private — so the handout carries a summary of each one along with the external references that note cites. Nothing hides behind a citation you cannot reach.

Nothing reaches this page until an automated scan has read every rendered page and document for material that was meant to stay private.
